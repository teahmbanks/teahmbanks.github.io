# AI Asset Research and Generation Record

## Dragonfly Logo

- **Date:** August 17, 2026
- **Tool:** OpenAI built-in image generator
- **Project use:** Personal logo in the shared portfolio header
- **Reference:** The hand-painted dragonfly photograph embedded in `My personal branding project - Google Docs.pdf` was used as a style and silhouette reference.
- **Prompt purpose:** Create a professional, vector-friendly dragonfly mark that retains the reference's elongated, imperfect hand-painted character and uses the portfolio's teal-and-gold palette.
- **Important constraints:** One centered dragonfly, no words or initials, no badge, no paper texture, no shadow, no watermark, and no additional objects.
- **Post-processing:** The generated flat magenta chroma-key background was removed locally to produce a transparent PNG.
- **Final asset:** `public/images/dragonfly-logo.png`

This generated mark is a branding asset. It is not presented as evidence of a completed project, employer, credential, award, or coding result.
